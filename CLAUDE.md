## What this repo is

lib-yahboom-gamepad: a published Wendoo library, the Yahboom GHBit gamepad driver for the micro:bit (v2): the
thumb-stick direction and continuous position sensors, the
colored-button sensor, the received-packet decoder, and the
position-to-packet radio encoding.
Its identity is the coordinate `wendoo-lang/lib-yahboom-gamepad`; consuming
applications approve it through their catalogs and install it at
pinned commits.
